How to Work Texture Into an Outfit

Texture can make even a simple outfit feel more interesting. You do not need bold patterns or complicated styling when different fabrics are already bringing subtle variation to the look.

Start With Familiar Fabrics

Texture is often easiest to introduce through pieces you already know how to wear. Denim, cotton, wool, linen, corduroy, suede, and knitted fabrics can all create different visual effects.

Instead of trying to combine several unusual textures at once, start with one textured piece and build around it with simpler fabrics.

Mix Smooth and Textured Pieces

One of the simplest combinations is contrast. A soft knitted layer can sit nicely with smooth trousers, while denim can provide structure next to a lightweight cotton shirt.

  • Pair chunky knitwear with smoother lower layers.
  • Use denim to add structure to softer fabrics.
  • Try linen alongside simple cotton pieces.
  • Use textured accessories when the main outfit is very simple.

Consider the Season

Texture naturally changes with the seasons. Heavier knits and brushed fabrics can create a cosy feel in cooler months, while linen and lighter woven fabrics can give warm-weather outfits a more relaxed character.

Do Not Overdo It

Too many competing textures can make an outfit feel visually busy. If one piece has a strong texture, surrounding it with simpler materials can help it stand out without overwhelming the rest of the look.

Use Accessories Carefully

Bags, belts, scarves, and shoes can introduce texture without changing the entire outfit. A woven bag or softly textured scarf can add another layer of interest to a straightforward combination.

Texture works best when it feels natural rather than deliberately complicated. Start with one or two contrasting fabrics and let their differences create the detail.
