Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers supporting the website or with third parties involved in an offer or service selected by a visitor. We do not sell personal information simply because you browse this website.

Gather & Grain does not control external websites linked from this website. Their privacy policies, cookies, security practices, and terms may differ from ours.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information handled through the website. No online system can guarantee complete security.

10. Your Choices

You may have the ability to manage cookies and certain tracking or advertising preferences through your browser, device, or applicable third-party controls.

11. Children's Privacy

Gather & Grain does not knowingly request personal information from children. If information is believed to have been submitted by a child, appropriate measures may be taken where applicable.

12. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by publishing a revised version on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the contact method made available on the Gather & Grain website.
