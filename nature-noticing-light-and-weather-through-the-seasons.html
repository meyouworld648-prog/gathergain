Noticing Light and Weather Through the Seasons

Familiar landscapes are never quite the same. Changes in light, cloud, rain, wind, and temperature can reveal different details throughout the year.

Light Changes More Than We Think

The position and quality of sunlight can alter the appearance of almost any outdoor space. A path that looks ordinary in midday may feel completely different in early morning or late afternoon.

Shadows can also reveal shapes and textures that are less obvious in brighter conditions.

Cloudy Days Have Their Own Character

Overcast weather creates softer, more even light. Colours, textures, and details can appear differently compared with a bright sunny day.

A cloudy afternoon can therefore be an opportunity to notice a familiar landscape without the strong contrasts created by direct sunlight.

Rain Changes the Details

Rain can bring out textures in paths, leaves, bark, stone, and other surfaces. Puddles can also reflect parts of the surrounding landscape that are normally viewed from a different angle.

  • Notice reflections after rainfall.
  • Look at how leaves and plants respond to moisture.
  • Watch how clouds alter the light.
  • Observe how wind changes trees and grasses.
  • Compare the same place across different days.

Discover More Outdoor Inspiration

Seasonal Light

As the year progresses, the angle and length of daylight change. This affects when certain places receive sunlight and how long evening light remains.

Weather Encourages Attention

Different conditions naturally encourage us to look more closely. Wind can reveal movement, rain can highlight texture, and clear skies can open up views.

Return to the Same Place

One of the best ways to appreciate these changes is to revisit a familiar place regularly. Over time, you begin to recognise subtle patterns that are easy to miss during a single visit.
